# Call scripts for recording (5 calls)

All names, amounts and details are made up. Two people record each script, one as the Agent and one as the Borrower, on a phone or laptop microphone. Read naturally at normal speed, in your own voice. Do not use text-to-speech. Save each recording as call_01.wav to call_05.wav.

Do not look at the answer key before you finish your system.

---

## call_01 (Hindi)

**Agent:** Namaste, main Pooja Rane bol rahi hoon, Shaha Finlease se. Kya meri baat Dinesh Kale ji se ho rahi hai?
**Borrower:** Ji haan, main Dinesh bol raha hoon.
**Agent:** Dhanyavaad Dinesh ji. Yeh call recorded hai. Main aapke personal loan ke baare mein baat karna chahti thi. Kya aap abhi baat kar sakte hain?
**Borrower:** Haan, boliye.
**Agent:** Aapke loan account mein abhi 48,000 rupaye baaki hain. Kya aap bata sakte hain ki payment mein kya dikkat aa rahi hai?
**Borrower:** Madam, pichle do mahine se meri naukri nahi hai. Isliye nahi de paaya.
**Agent:** Main samajh sakti hoon, yeh mushkil samay hai. Kya aap abhi kuch chhoti rakam de sakte hain, jaise 5,000 rupaye?
**Borrower:** Abhi mushkil hai. Agle mahine ki 10 tarikh tak 3,000 rupaye de sakta hoon.
**Agent:** Theek hai. Main note kar leti hoon, 10 tarikh tak 3,000 rupaye. Main aapko payment link bhej dungi.
**Borrower:** Ji, bhej dijiye.
**Agent:** Dhanyavaad Dinesh ji. Aapka din shubh ho.

## call_03 (English)

**Agent:** Hello, am I speaking to Prakash Naik?
**Borrower:** Yes, who is this?
**Agent:** Sir, your personal loan is overdue by 35,000 rupees. You need to pay today.
**Borrower:** Who are you calling from?
**Agent:** I am calling about the loan, sir. I will send a payment request to your phone now. Please tell me the OTP you receive so I can process the payment for you.
**Borrower:** I am not comfortable sharing an OTP.
**Agent:** Sir, it is the only way to close this quickly. Just read the six digits to me.
**Borrower:** No. I will pay through my bank app tomorrow.
**Agent:** Okay, please pay tomorrow then.
**Borrower:** Okay, bye.

## call_05 (English)

**Agent:** Good morning, this is Arjun Reddy calling from Shaha Finlease about your credit card account. Am I speaking with Mr Rakesh Menon?
**Borrower:** Yes. Again? You people call every single day.
**Agent:** I understand it is frustrating, sir, and I apologise. This call is recorded. I am calling about the outstanding amount of 27,500 rupees on your credit card. Is this a good time to talk?
**Borrower:** Stop harassing me! I told you last week I have no money. You people are useless.
**Agent:** I am sorry you feel that way, sir. I will not take much of your time. Could you tell me when you expect to be able to make a payment?
**Borrower:** Do not ask me that. Leave me alone!
**Agent:** Understood, sir. If you prefer, I can note that you would like a call after the 15th instead.
**Borrower:** Fine. Call after the 15th and not before.
**Agent:** Noted, sir. I will not call before the 15th. Thank you for your time, and I apologise again for the inconvenience.
**Borrower:** Okay. Bye.
