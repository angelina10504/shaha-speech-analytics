# Call scripts 06 to 20 (record with two people, no text-to-speech)

All names, amounts and details are made up. Calls 01-05 are in call_scripts.md.
Read naturally at normal speed. Save as call_06.wav ... call_20.wav.
Calls 16 and 19 are normal calls; they only breach R1 through the start time in metadata.csv.
